FEATURED RESEARCH / 17 OCT 2026

FRT: The Puzzle Solver

CFA Institute Research Challenge 2026-27 · Ton Duc Thang University · National Round

Investment Thesis

Long Chau retains its competitive advantage, but expansion capacity in core cities is narrowing. The research examines provincial expansion and the role of sales per store in long-term growth.

RECOMMENDATIONHOLD
TARGET PRICE155,300 VND
UPSIDE AT REPORT DATE+10.46%

Research Framework

  • A spatial model estimates pharmacy market capacity across 34 provinces.
  • Regional forecasts across six regions link store openings to market capacity.
  • Valuation combines FCFF, FCFE and relative valuation.
  • Monte Carlo simulation with 1,000,000 runs assesses the distribution of valuation outcomes.
  • Risk analysis covers competition, costs, store locations, pharmacist availability and cannibalisation.

My contribution

Industry & Macroeconomic Research, Spatial Model, Partial Valuation, Monte Carlo Simulation, Risk Analysis and Report Writing.

Reflections from the report

Consider combining FCFF with EV/EBITDA, expanding regulatory and FX risk coverage, and linking ESG to financial materiality.

These findings are team research outputs. Reference price: VND 140,600 on October 14, 2026. Report date: October 17, 2026. Student work for an educational competition, not investment advice or a current recommendation.
